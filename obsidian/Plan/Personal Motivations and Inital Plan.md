 1. I want to be able to use the CoV to solve optimisation problems.
 2. I want to learn the direct method in order to solve more problems in CoV.
 3. Explore Numerical Methods if time allows.

## Dissertation Plan (12 Weeks)

### Weeks 1–4: Learn the Direct Method
- Study the Direct Method through textbooks and lecture notes.
- Learn the necessary prerequisites (functional analysis, weak convergence, Sobolev spaces, etc.) to a working level.

### Weeks 5–6: Literature Exploration
- Review textbooks, papers, and lecture notes.
- Investigate applications of the Calculus of Variations.
- Identify problems that have been solved using variational methods.

### Weeks 7–10: Problem Investigation
- Reproduce known results using CoV techniques.
- Explore extensions or variations of existing problems.
- If feasible, formulate and investigate an original problem.

### Weeks 11–12: Writing and Refinement
- Complete the dissertation write-up.
- Refine proofs, notation, and presentation.
- Final proofreading and formatting.

### Notes
- Writing will take place throughout the project rather than being left until the final two weeks.
