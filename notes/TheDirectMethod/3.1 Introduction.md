We study the problem
$$
\inf \left\{ I(u) = \int_\Omega f(x,u(x), \nabla u(x)) \, dx : u \in u_0 + W_0^{1,p} \right\} = m
$$
where
- $\Omega \subset \mathbb{R}^n$ is a bounded open set;
- $f : \overline\Omega \times \mathbb{R} \times \mathbb{R}^n \rightarrow \mathbb{R}, f = f(x,u,\xi);$
- $u \in u_0 + W_0^{1,p}(\Omega)$ means that $u,u_0\in W_0^{1,p}(\Omega)$ (roughly means that $u=u_0$ on $\partial \Omega$).

We will show that the problem has a so the problem has a solution $\overline{u} \in U_0 + W_0^{1,p}(\Omega)$  provided the two following main hypotheses are satisfied.

1. *convexity*: $\xi \rightarrow f(x,u,\xi)$ is convex for every $(x,u) \in \overline{\Omega} \times \mathbb{R};$
2. *Coercivity*: there exist $p>q \ge 1$ and $\alpha_1 >0, \alpha_2,\alpha_3 \in \mathbb{R}$ such that
$$
f(x,u,\xi) \ge \alpha_1|\xi|^p + \alpha_2|u|^q + \alpha_3, \quad \forall(x,u,\xi) \in \overline{\Omega} \times \mathbb{R} \times \mathbb{R}^n.
$$

The *Dirichlet integral* which has integrand
$$
f(x,u,\xi) = \frac{1}{2}|\xi|^2
$$
satisfies both hypotheses.

However the *minimal surface* problem whose integrand is given by
$$
f(x,u, \xi) = \sqrt{1+|\xi|^2}
$$
satisfies Convexity but verifies Coercivity only with $p =1$
